# Task T4a — other minds & social influence, language, culture  → write `tools/psy_qa/facts/FACTS_T4a.md`
Read `tools/psy_qa/briefs/RESEARCH.md` first. Lessons covered: **12** reading minds & social influence, **13** language,
**14** culture. Story: the hardest thing to predict is another predictor (theory of mind); others' judgments are evidence
(conformity); words move models from one brain to another (language); the moved models become local priors (culture) —
and most findings were gathered from WEIRD samples. Several famous social-psychology items need correction.

- ★ Statistical learning: Saffran, Aslin & Newport 1996 *Science* (8-month-olds; 2 minutes; the four words

- ★ "Word gap" folklore: Hart & Risley 1995 ("30 million words") — critiques and failed replication (Sperry, Sperry & Miller
