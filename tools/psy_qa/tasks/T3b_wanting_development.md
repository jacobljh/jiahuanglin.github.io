# Task T3b — wanting vs liking (habits, addiction), development  → write `tools/psy_qa/facts/FACTS_T3b.md`
Read `tools/psy_qa/briefs/RESEARCH.md` first. Lessons covered: **10** wanting ≠ liking (dopamine, habits, addiction),
**11** development (how the machine is built from zero). Story: learning needs a start state and a rate; wanting (cue-triggered
motivation) and liking (hedonic impact) are separable systems and can come apart (addiction, compulsive checking); and the
developing brain is a learner whose priors, windows and relationships are assembled over time. Heritability is NOT destiny.

## 10 · wanting ≠ liking (≈85 bullets)
- ★ Berridge & Robinson 1998 *Brain Res Rev* "What is the role of dopamine in reward: hedonic impact, reward learning, or
  incentive salience?" — the 6-OHDA depletion experiments: taste-reactivity ("liking") intact, motivation ("wanting") gone;
  numbers if available. Berridge 2007 *Psychopharmacology* (the debate); Berridge & Kringelbach 2015 *Neuron* "Pleasure
  systems in the brain" (hedonic hotspots: nucleus accumbens shell ~1 mm³ rat, ventral pallidum; opioid/endocannabinoid);
  Peciña & Berridge 2005; Smith & Berridge 2007; Castro & Berridge 2014. Grade: the wanting/liking dissociation in animals A/B;
  in humans: Leyton et al. 2002 (amphetamine dopamine release vs wanting), Finlayson, King & Blundell 2007 (food), Pecina 2003 — B/C.
- Zhang, Berridge et al. 2009 (salt appetite: wanting appears without learned association) — "instant wanting".
- Incentive-sensitization theory: Robinson & Berridge 1993 *Brain Res Rev*, 2008 update; evidence in humans (Boileau 2006;
  Leyton 2002; Evans 2006 'Sensitization of cue-induced craving'); critiques (Hogarth; Volkow & Wise; Berridge's reply);
  Grade B/C.
- Cue-triggered motivation: Pavlovian-to-instrumental transfer (Estes 1943; Holmes, Marchand & Coutureau 2010 review),
  sign-tracking vs goal-tracking rats (Flagel, Akil & Robinson 2009; Robinson & Flagel 2009; human analogues—Garofalo & di
  Pellegrino 2015?) — exact definitions and numbers (e.g., % sign-trackers).
- Habits: Dickinson 1985, Adams & Dickinson 1981 (outcome devaluation), Daw, Niv & Dayan 2005 (arbitration), Graybiel 2008
  Annu Rev Neurosci, Wood & Rünger 2016 *Annu Rev Psychol* "Psychology of habit"; ★ Lally, van Jaarsveld, Potts & Wardle 2010
  *Eur J Soc Psychol* (96 volunteers; median 66 days to automaticity; range 18–254; model: asymptote curve—give the fitted
  formula/parameters if stated); Gardner, Lally & Wardle 2012; Wood, Quinn & Kashy 2002 (diary: ~43% of daily behaviours
  repeated in the same context); de Wit et al. 2018 (human habit-learning evidence is weaker than rodent: critical
  reviews—Buabang 2021) → grade B/C; also Wood, Mazar & Neal 2022 "Habits and goals in human behavior".
- Addiction: Everitt & Robbins 2005 (goal-directed → habitual → compulsive) and critiques; Hart 2013 / Heyman 2013 (choice

## 11 · development (≈110 bullets)
A. The child as learner (the "prediction error in babies" thread)
- Baillargeon, Spelke & Wasserman 1985 (5-month-olds, violation-of-expectation); Spelke 1990/2007 core knowledge; critique &
  replication status (Charles & Rivera 2009? Cashon; Kagan; Many Babies: ManyBabies 1 (2020) infant-directed speech preference
  replicates; ManyBabies 2 theory-of-mind anticipatory-looking replication — **Schuwerk et al. 2024/2025 results**) → grade C for
  precise violation-of-expectation inferences; B for infants as statistical learners.
- ★ Kidd, Piantadosi & Aslin 2012 PLoS ONE "The Goldilocks effect"; Stahl & Feigenson 2015 *Science* "Observing the unexpected
  enhances infants' learning and exploration"; Xu & Garcia 2008 PNAS (8-month-olds infer sample→population); Gweon, Tenenbaum
  & Schulz 2010 PNAS; Gopnik & Wellman 2012 *Psych Bull* "Reconstructing constructivism"; Gopnik et al. 2004; Saffran, Aslin &
  Newport 1996 (details in T4a — only a pointer here) → grade A/B for statistical learning, B/C for "child as scientist".
B. Piaget & stages
- Piaget's four stages (dates/ages), object permanence (A-not-B error; Piaget 1954; Baillargeon 1985; Thelen & Smith 1994,
  Smith & Thelen 2003) — what survives (sequence and error patterns) vs what does not (ages, discontinuous stages;
  Flavell 1963; Siegler 1996 'overlapping waves'); conservation tasks (Piaget & Inhelder 1941; McGarrigle & Donaldson 1974
  "naughty teddy"); Vygotsky ZPD (1978) and scaffolding (Wood, Bruner & Ross 1976) → B.
C. Theory of mind (short; T4a covers detail)
- Wellman, Cross & Watson 2001 (178 studies); Callaghan et al. 2005 (five cultures: same sequence, age varies); Liu, Wellman
  et al. 2008 (Beijing vs Canada: knowledge-access before diverse belief); Onishi & Baillargeon 2005 and replication
  failures (Poulin-Dubois et al. 2018 review "Do infants understand false beliefs? We don't know yet"; Kulke et al. 2018;
  Dörrenberg et al. 2018) → grade C for infant implicit false belief.
D. Attachment
- Harlow 1958 (contact comfort); Bowlby 1969; Ainsworth et al. 1978 *Patterns of Attachment* (Strange Situation:
  procedure with 8 episodes; classic proportions ~ 65–70% B, 20% A, 10–15% C in US low-risk samples); Main & Solomon 1986
  disorganized (≈15%); van IJzendoorn & Kroonenberg 1988 cross-cultural meta (32 studies, 8 countries); Fraley 2002
  *Pers Soc Psychol Rev* (stability) and Fraley & Roisman 2019; Groh et al. 2014/2017 (secure attachment & later social
  competence: small effects d≈0.1–0.3?); Sroufe et al. Minnesota longitudinal; Rutter's English & Romanian Adoptees study;
  ★ Bucharest Early Intervention Project (Nelson, Zeanah & Fox 2007 *Science*; Zeanah 2011; 2019 update) — numbers on IQ and
  attachment by placement age (before/after 24 months) → grade A/B for severe early deprivation. Contested: attachment
  styles in adult romantic relationships (Fraley 2019; Hazan & Shaver 1987) — grade C for popular "attachment style" typing
  (state measurement issues: dimensional anxiety/avoidance).
E. Critical/sensitive periods (the series' best-evidenced "windows")
- Hubel & Wiesel 1963/1970 (kitten monocular deprivation: sensitive period weeks 4–6; Nobel 1981); human amblyopia
  (Lewis & Maurer 2005; Birch 2013; Holmes & Clarke 2006: treatment works to older ages — PEDIG trials show improvement up to
  age 17, so "windows" are softer than textbooks claim) → B; language (T4a); Genie (T4a); music/absolute pitch (Deutsch 2006?;
  Baharloo 1998 early training) → C/B; hearing (cochlear implant age thresholds: Kral & Sharma 2012) → A/B.
F. Nature × nurture
- ★ Polderman et al. 2015 *Nature Genetics* meta-analysis (2,748 publications, 17,804 traits, ~14.5M twin pairs; average

  epigenetic inheritance in humans is unproven — Horsthemke 2018) → state carefully.
