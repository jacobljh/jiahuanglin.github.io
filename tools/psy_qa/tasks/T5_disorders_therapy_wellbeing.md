- Distress + impairment as the working criteria (DSM "clinically significant distress or impairment") [def, source]; Rosenhan 1973

- Prevalence (careful, year-stamped, definition-stamped): Kessler et al. 2005 (NCS-R: lifetime any disorder 46.4%, 12-month 26.2%);

B. Mechanism: loops, attractors, networks

## 18 · therapy (≈105 bullets)
- Effectiveness: ★ Cuijpers et al. 2023 *World Psychiatry* "Psychological treatment of depression: a systematic overview of
  meta-analyses" (pooled d≈0.7 vs control; adjusted for bias ≈0.5?) exact; Cuijpers 2019 on publication bias; Munder et al. 2019
  (allegiance effects); Smith & Glass 1977 (first meta-analysis, d≈0.68) and Eysenck's 1952 critique; Shedler 2010 (psychodynamic
  d≈0.97 criticised); Leichsenring/ Steinert 2017 (psychodynamic therapy meta); Wampold & Imel 2015 *The Great Psychotherapy
  Debate* → common factors; Flückiger et al. 2018 *Psychotherapy* (alliance–outcome r=.278; 295 independent studies, N≈30,000)
  ; Norcross & Lambert 2018 (what works in relationships); Baldwin & Imel 2013 (therapist effects ≈5–8% of variance); Rosenzweig
  1936 "dodo bird verdict", Luborsky 1975, Wampold 1997, and the critiques (Tolin 2010: CBT beats other therapies for anxiety,
  d≈0.1–0.4?). Expectancy/hope (Frank & Frank 1991). Grade: therapy works A; "no differences between therapies" C/B.
- ★ Behavioural activation vs CBT: Jacobson et al. 1996 (component analysis), Dimidjian et al. 2006 *J Consult Clin Psychol* (N=241:
  BA≈antidepressant>cognitive therapy in severe), Richards et al. 2016 *Lancet* COBRA (N=440, non-inferiority; costs), Cuijpers,
  van Straten & Warmerdam 2007 meta (BA ≈ CT). → so "cognitive restructuring" is not the unique lever (A/B).
- ★ Exposure & extinction learning: Foa & Kozak 1986; Craske et al. 2008, 2014 (inhibitory learning; expectancy violation) and tests
  (Rowa et al. 2014; Deacon et al. 2013; Craske 2022 review "Optimizing exposure"); the role of prediction error in extinction
  (Rescorla–Wagner; Quirk & Mueller 2008; Bouton); contested: does expectancy violation predict outcome? (Asnaani et al. 2015?;
  Papalini 2020?) → B/C; Exposure therapy vs medication for anxiety; Carpenter et al. 2018 meta (CBT anxiety children/adults effect).
- Behavioural experiments: Bennett-Levy et al. 2004 (book); McMillan & Lee 2010; Rouf, Fennell, Westbrook... → B.
- Cognitive therapy evidence: Beck 1979; Hollon, Stewart & Strunk 2006 (CT vs meds relapse prevention); Hofmann et al. 2012 review of
  CBT meta-analyses; Hofmann & Smits 2008; mediators (Kazantzis 2018?): do cognitive changes precede symptom change? (Lorenzo-Luaces
  et al. 2015; Burns & Spangler 2001) → C.
- Medications (descriptive only, no advice): ★ Cipriani et al. 2018 *Lancet* (21 antidepressants, 522 trials, 116,477 participants:
  all more effective than placebo, OR 1.37–2.13; effect size SMD≈0.3); Kirsch et al. 2008 PLoS Med; Fournier 2010 JAMA (severity);
  Munkholm 2019?; Moncrieff 2022 (serotonin); placebo response rates; relapse on discontinuation (Hengartner?) ; Cuijpers 2014 combined

- Delivery: internet CBT (Andersson 2014; Karyotaki 2017 individual patient data: guided iCBT ≈ face-to-face), apps (Linardon

- Harmful / ineffective: critical incident stress debriefing (above), recovered-memory therapy (Loftus; Lilienfeld 2007 "Psychological
