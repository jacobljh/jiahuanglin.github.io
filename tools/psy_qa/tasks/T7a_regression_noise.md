Read `tools/psy_qa/briefs/RESEARCH.md`, then `tasks/T7_COMMON.md` (it overrides where it differs), then `tools/psy_qa/PLAN_EXT.md`.
Story. 07: every measurement = stable part + luck; when you select on an extreme first measurement the second one is on average
closer to the mean *with no cause at all*; the brain invents a cause (praise/punishment, treatments, speed cameras, "jinxes").
Lesson 07 teaches the mechanism, how big the artefact can be (1 − r), the famous misreadings, and how research designs defend
against it (controls, randomisation, baseline-free designs). 08: the same judge, same case, different day/ordering → different
judgment (noise); judgment error = bias² + noise² (an identity); mechanical rules and averaging remove noise; "decision hygiene".
Names below are suggestions from memory — **verify every one, numbers included; drop or correct what you cannot confirm.**

- ★ Averaging cancels noise but not bias: Galton (exists), Vul & Pashler 2008 *Psych Sci* "Measuring the crowd within" (N, the gain from averaging two guesses 3 weeks apart: the reduction in error), Herzog & Hertwig 2009 dialectical bootstrapping, Larrick & Soll 2006 *Management Science* "Intuitions about combining opinions: misappreciation of the averaging principle" (bracketing; people prefer picking the best — the numbers), Rauhut & Lorenz 2011; replication status of the crowd-within effect (Steegen/Dewitte/Vanpaemel 2014; Hourihan & Benjamin 2010).
- ★ Formulas ≥ experts: Meehl 1954 (what it argued; the 'broken leg'), Grove 2000 (exists), Dawes 1979 "The robust beauty of improper linear models" (find abstract), Kuncel 2013 (exists), Kleinberg et al. 2018 QJE "Human decisions and machine predictions" (bail decisions: algorithm vs judges), Dietvorst/Simmons/Massey 2015 *JEP:G* algorithm aversion + replication/boundary (Jussupow 2020; Mahmud 2022) — grade each.
- ★ Structured vs unstructured interviews (noise reduction in practice): Sackett, Zhang, Berry & Lievens 2022 *J Appl Psych* "Revisiting meta-analytic estimates of validity in personnel selection" (structured vs unstructured interview validity), Schmidt & Hunter 1998, Conway/Jako/Goodman 1995 (interview reliability), Dana/Dawes/Peterson 2013 (belief in the unstructured interview). Numbers.
- Reliability of expert judgment in other domains (wine-competition consistency Hodgson 2008/2009; credit/lending; clinical psychologists' agreement — Garb 1989; Oskamp 1965 (more information → more confidence, no more accuracy)) — only what you can verify; grade.
- Judges and "irrelevant" influences: Danziger (exists, D) and the other direction: Eren & Mocan 2018 (football losses & juvenile sentences), Heyes & Saberian 2019 (temperature & asylum decisions), Chen/Moskowitz/Shue 2016 (gambler's fallacy in asylum/loan/umpire decisions); Chen & Loecher? — verify, grade honestly (many are C/B).
- Decision hygiene / noise reduction techniques with evidence: structured judgment, independent assessment, delayed intuition (Kahneman's "mediating assessments protocol"), checklists (Gawande/Haynes 2009 NEJM WHO surgical safety checklist: complications 11.0% → 7.0%, deaths 1.5% → 0.8%; later critiques) — verify.
