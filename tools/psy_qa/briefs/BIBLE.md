## §0 Red lines (read twice)

1. **Facts come only from the FACTS files.** Every number, date, name, quote, study, sample size, effect size and "later it was shown that…" in prose must be a `[V]` item (or a `[~]` item phrased the way the file recommends, with the range and who says it). Anything under a file's "COULD NOT VERIFY" is **banned**. If you need a fact that isn't there, write around it (qualitatively, no number, no name) — do not look it up and do not use memory. Quotes: only the exact wording in FACTS. **Old lessons are not sources**: when you port a paragraph from the old series, every fact in it must be re-found in FACTS or cut.
2. **Evidence grades are not yours to choose.** Every named study or quantified result carries `<span class="ev ev-X" data-k="KEY">X</span>`; `KEY` must exist in `GRADES.json` and the letter must equal the recorded grade (verify.js checks). If the study you want has no key, you do not have the fact — write around it. In prose say in plain words what happened afterwards ("后来多个实验室重复，没有重复出来"). Grade definitions (§4) are fixed.
3. **Widget numbers are model outputs, not facts.** Every widget is a deterministic toy model; prose that quotes a widget number must say it is the model's (示意) and must be what the widget really prints (check with kpi.js). A model *inspired by* a paper is 「受某某启发的简化模型」, never the authors' equation. Where a widget MEASURES something from the reader (a reaction time, a recall rate), say so and never compare it to a norm that isn't in FACTS.
4. **Stance and care.** Educational and descriptive. No diagnosis, no treatment advice, no "do this" medical instructions; mental-health lessons (17–19) report evidence and point to professional care in one neutral sentence. **Never include method details for self-harm.** Group differences (intelligence, gender, culture, race): report only what the consensus statements and large meta-analyses in FACTS say; separate description from causal claims; no essentialism; heritability is a population statistic, not a statement about an individual or a group. Culture and politics: flat, sourced, no side-taking adjectives.
5. **People are pronoun-neutral:** never 他/她 (verify.js errors on them, including 他们/她们). Use the name, 「研究者」「受试者」「这位作者」「对方」「此人」, or restructure. 「其他」「他人」「他国」「利他」「他心」 are fine. For people in general use 「你」「人们」「我们」.
6. **No LaTeX.** Math is Unicode in `<span class="math">` / `<p class="ascii">`; sub/superscripts via `<sub>/<sup>`.
7. **Second person, plain Chinese, short sentences.** Explain every term the first time (中文名＋英文，如「预测误差（prediction error）」). No 黑话, no 鸡汤, no "研究表明" without saying which study.
8. **Linear thinking is the point:** every section must be *forced* by the previous one. If a section could move elsewhere without breaking anything, it is in the wrong place. The lesson answers its incoming baton and ends by raising its outgoing baton. Write each lesson as: the previous design fails → what it would take → the mechanism → a number you can check → where the mechanism breaks → the next failure.
9. **Theories are lenses, not laws.** Say what a model predicts, what evidence supports it (FACTS, with grades), and where it fails. One 「这个模型的边界」 paragraph per model lesson. Present contested ideas as contested (C), failed ones as cautionary tales (D) — a lesson that teaches a D-grade result as fact is a defect.
10. The engine wording is fixed (§1). Echo it in every lesson's 回到主线 callout.

## §1 Engine and throughlines

**Engine (state it in 00 and the finale; echo in every 回到主线) — pronoun-free, use verbatim:**
> 心智是一台有边界的推断机器：它的任务，是替一具必须活下去的身体，在一群同类中间，用有限的算力、时间和能量，做出「够用」的猜测。所以读懂任何一种心理现象，只要问五个问题：它在猜什么（先验与证据）、拿什么打分（价值）、为省力做了什么取舍（成本）、按什么误差更新（学习）、旁边还有谁在一起猜（他人）。

**Throughlines (name them where they recur):**
- **① 猜（预测）：** 感官给的原料又少又糊，所以必须猜；真正携带信息的，是猜错的那一部分——预测误差（03 → 09 → 17 → 18）。
- **② 成本（够用）：** 算力、能量、时间都有账单。「捷径」「偏误」「遗忘」「注意力窄」不是设计缺陷，而是在成本约束下的取舍；「够用」在一定条件下是一个可以推出来的最优（偏差—方差权衡，取决于环境和数据量），不是一句安慰（04 → 05 → 06 → 08）。
- **③ 价值（打分）：** 没有打分标准就谈不上「更好」。身体的需要给猜测打分，情绪是分数的读数，学习是按分数改模型，动机是分数的预期（07 → 09 → 10 → 19）。
- **④ 他人：** 别的脑袋既是重要的信息来源，也是重要的变量；语言把一个脑子的模型送进另一个，文化是一群脑子共享的先验（11 → 12 → 13 → 14 → 15）。
- **⑤ 证据（我们怎么知道）：** 每个结论都带着「有多稳」。这条线从 01、02 起头，贯穿全程：A/B/C/D 徽章就是它的可见形态，19 把它收成一张账本。

Relation to siblings: 《计算心智》 holds the formal treatment (Bayesian brain, predictive coding, free energy, RL & dopamine, computational psychiatry) — this series teaches the *phenomena and the evidence* and cites those lessons for the math; 《哲学》 holds the metaphysics (mind–body, other minds, free will, personal identity) — psychology reports what is measured and stops there.

## §4 Evidence discipline (the series' own contribution)

Grades — fixed definitions, they become the badges' tooltips:
- **A 稳健** — several independent teams, adequate samples (or a registered/large multi-lab replication, or a large meta-analysis) agree; direction and rough size hold up.
- **B 成立但有限定** — direction is reliable, but size or boundary conditions are disputed / vary by sample, or it has been replicated only in limited settings.
- **C 有争议** — mixed or thin replications; strong alternative explanations; do not treat as a conclusion.
- **D 站不住** — large or pre-registered replications failed, retracted, or substantially revised: teach as a caution.
A classic gets the grade of its **current** evidence, not its fame.

Rules for the badge:
1. One `.ev` on every named study / quantified empirical claim at its first appearance in a section, and in every `.source-note` of type 研究小注 and `.bet-reveal`. A study may appear in several lessons; use the same `data-k`.
2. Markup is exactly `<span class="ev ev-a" data-k="KEY">A</span>` (letter lower-case in the class, upper-case in the text). Do not add a `title` (psy.js adds the tooltip "A 稳健 / B 成立但有限定 / C 有争议 / D 站不住"); lesson 02 carries the full legend. Do not invent keys.
3. In prose, the badge is never the only explanation of a failed replication: say in a sentence what was tried and what came out.
4. Do not grade definitions, history, or mechanisms.
5. Each lesson from 03 on has ≥ 3 badges; at least one lesson-level 「这个结果有多稳」 sentence tells the reader which of the lesson's claims are A and which are shaky.

## §5 The 押注 (prediction-first bet)

Every lesson except 19 opens (after 本课路线) with one bet: the reader commits to a number or a pick **before** the lesson reveals it. Why: pretesting and hypercorrection effects (FACTS_T6a) — a wrong confident guess is corrected better than a passively read fact; and lesson 19 replays all bets as a calibration scoreboard.
- Pick a question whose answer (a) is in FACTS as a `[V]` number or a clear fact, (b) is mildly surprising, (c) is answered in this lesson. Never bet on a contested/sensitive number (group differences, suicide, trauma prevalence); bet on mechanisms and well-attested effects.
- `data-kind="num"` (slider; set `data-min/max/step/unit`, `data-truth`, `data-tol` = what counts as 命中, default 10% of range) or `data-kind="pick"` (`data-options="A|B|C"`, `data-truth` = index). `data-id` = lesson number + letter (`03a`); sidecar ids start with `p` (`p01a`). `psy.js` renders the controls and hides `.bet-reveal` until the reader locks a guess.
- The reveal states the truth, the source, the badge, and which section derives it. It must read correctly whether the guess was right or wrong (no "你猜错了").

## §6 Widget conventions

- Exactly one `.widget` with: `.title`, `.hint` (tell the reader exactly what to try and what they'll see — must be TRUE for the model; verify with kpi.js), `.controls` (buttons / `label.inline` sliders with a `.val` span), `.kpi-grid` of 3–5 `.kpi` (`<div class="kpi"><div class="k">标签</div><div class="v" id="wNN-x">—</div></div>`), one `<canvas id="wNN-canvas" class="dist-canvas">`, and optionally `<div class="hint" id="wNN-note" style="margin-top:8px">—</div>` narrating the current state in one sentence.
- **Every element id starts with `wNN-`** (verify enforces it).
- Vanilla JS in one trailing IIFE, `var` style, no libraries. **No Math.random / Date** — use `PSY.rng(seed)` (seeded, from `psy.js`) for noise. Real-time measurement widgets (reaction time) may read `performance.now()`; they must also run (deterministically) when it returns a constant (the headless stub). DPR-aware canvas via `PSY.fit(canvas, cssHeight)`, a taller stacked layout below 560 px, redraw on `resize`. Palette: `PSY.C` (light panel; amber `#d97706`, green `#16a34a`, blue `#2563eb`, red `#dc2626`, slate text). Font `11px sans-serif` labels.
- **The widget must COMPUTE or MEASURE the lesson's mechanism** (a formula, an optimization, a small simulation, or the reader's own response), not illustrate a fixed picture, and the interesting behaviour must *emerge* from the model rather than be put in by hand. **If the spec's model is wrong or degenerate (no interior optimum, a branch that never happens), fix the model so the teaching point really appears — and say what you changed in your final message.** Prototype the maths in node first (`tools/psy_qa/proto/NN.js`) and make the widget print the same numbers.
- Label schematic parts 示意; constants that come from the literature are cited (FACTS) in the code comment; tuned constants are documented in the code comment.
- KPI text never shows NaN/undefined/Infinity. Chinese labels and notes. Keep canvas text inside the canvas at 320 px width (layout.js after every preset/button state).
