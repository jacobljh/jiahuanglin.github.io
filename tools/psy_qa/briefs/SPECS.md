## 05 · 记忆：不是录像，是每次重新建构的故事
**Job.** What passes the gate is stored — but not as a recording. **Port from:** old `05_memory.html` (the misinformation widget idea) — rebuild.

**Sections.**
1. 一、记忆不是回放 — Bartlett in one honest paragraph: what he claimed vs the textbook version (✗ "always distorted"); Bergman & Roediger 1999 replicated the repeated-reproduction pattern; Wynn & Logie 1998 found accuracy was maintained for real personal events; portrait series not replicated (D).
