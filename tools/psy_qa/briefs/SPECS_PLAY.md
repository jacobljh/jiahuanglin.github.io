## Common to ALL sidecar lessons

**What this series is.** 《心理学》 explains WHY the mind works as it does; 《从零学会用脑》 asks which practical techniques have evidence, how big, for whom, how long, and what the strongest criticism is — then hands the reader ONE small self-experiment. Its spine is one sentence: **知道 ≠ 用得上；先问证据，再问怎么做；一次只改一件事，用自己的数据说话。** Evidence-first means popular-but-weak techniques are named and graded too (rereading, highlighting, learning styles, "sleep on it", ego-depletion tricks…).
